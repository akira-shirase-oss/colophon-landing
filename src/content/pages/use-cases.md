Each page below starts from a job a team needs a CMS to do, says where the usual way of doing it goes wrong on
any CMS, and then shows how Colophon does it, with the details from the docs. Colophon is in alpha, as the
status line above says.

## The use cases

| The job | Page |
|---|---|
| AI agents write drafts, people approve and publish | [Let AI agents draft content, and people publish](/use-cases/ai-agents-draft-people-publish/) |
| Answer what was live, who approved it and who could change it | [A CMS with an audit trail for every publish](/use-cases/audited-publishing/) |
| Put articles behind a sign-in without leaking them | [Members-only content on a headless CMS](/use-cases/members-only-content/) |
| Rebuild a static site on every publish, and check it changed | [Keep a static site fresh after every publish](/use-cases/static-site-fresh-after-publish/) |

The features these rely on, such as publish receipts and reviewed rollback, each have a page in
[Colophon's features](/features/).
